# local-ai-automation-framework 🚀

**Framework for implementing autonomous AI agents and operational automations for SMEs in Cuernavaca, Mexico, focused on democratizing AI for local healthcare and service businesses.**

This project is a specialized framework designed to implement autonomous AI agents and operational automations for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs/PyMEs) in Cuernavaca, Morelos.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The goal is to democratize access to advanced AI for local businesses, specifically targeting the healthcare and service sectors. By reducing the gap between high-end AI capabilities and budget-constrained entrepreneurs, this framework focuses on:

*   **Lead Recovery:** Automated WhatsApp/Instagram response systems.
*   **Appointment Setting:** Intelligent scheduling and qualification agents.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Transforming unstructured business data into actionable CRM entries.

---

## 🛠️ Technical & Operational Stack

My implementation framework is built on a high-performance AI infrastructure designed for scalability, reliability, and cost-optimization:

- **Multi-Agent Architecture:** Orchestration of autonomous systems using **Hermes Agent**, **Google Antigravity IDE 2.0**, and **Kiro IDE** for complex task delegation.
- **LLM Management & Optimization:** Advanced control of requests, response tuning, and token/cost optimization across **Anthropic**, **OpenAI**, and **Open Source models (Hugging Face)** tailored to specific business use cases.
- **Integration & Deterministic Workflows:** Construction of robust pipelines and **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** systems utilizing **n8n**, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, and **Google Workspace CLI**.
- **LLMOps & Observability:** Backend development and deployment focused on stability and monitoring using **Python**, **FastAPI**, **SQLite**, and a combination of **Vibe Coding & SDD (Specification-Driven Development)**.
- **Frameworks & Version Control:** Leveraging **Hugging Face**, **TensorFlow**, and **Git/GitHub** for production-grade environment management.
